Data Deletion Instructions

Gorani Toon Autopilot processes only public comments (text and public username) left on Gorani Toon posts, for the purpose of replying.

To request deletion of your comment data, email cgwkdal@gmail.com with:

  • the platform (Instagram / Threads / YouTube)
  • your public username

We will delete the stored comment data within 30 days of your request and confirm by email. You can delete the original comment directly on each platform.
